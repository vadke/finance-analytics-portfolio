Method

Income statement, balance sheet and cash flow figures for each company's last five fiscal years were taken from 10-K filings (as compiled by StockAnalysis.com) and loaded into SQLite. SQL checks confirm free cash flow ties to operating cash flow less capex, gross profit sits below revenue, operating income sits below gross profit, and all three statements join one-to-one.

Ratios are computed in one SQL view using window functions: year-over-year growth and inventory turnover use LAG over the prior year, and CAGR runs from each company's first year in the set. Fiscal years are labeled by the calendar year in which most of the period falls. Inventory turnover is cost of goods sold over average inventory; ROA is net income over average total assets.
