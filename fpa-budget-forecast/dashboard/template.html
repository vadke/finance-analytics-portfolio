    Method

    The company and its numbers are simulated: a seeded Python script generates 33 months of actuals and three annual budgets across four regions and two channels, with margins set near those of public big-box retailers. Built-in events include an e-commerce shift, a Northeast store slowdown, a freight cost spike and a spring marketing overspend.

    SQL views in SQLite roll the ledger into a P&L and compute variance by month, line and segment. The revenue forecast is a log-linear trend with month-of-year effects, fitted separately for each region and channel on the last 24 months and summed. Each department's cost is modeled as a fixed monthly amount plus a variable rate on revenue, fitted on the trailing 12 months. The same drivers feed an Excel model with a scenario selector and 500+ live formulas.
